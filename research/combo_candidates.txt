#1. CB — Compression Breakout (BASELINE) ✅
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Signal: 3-bar squeeze (max range < 0.7×ATR) on 5m → breakout direction
Entry:  Next-bar confirmation (high+0.1 BUY / low-0.1 SELL)
Filter: ATR 2.5-4.5 (AM) / 2.0-5.0 (PM), RSI<70, dedup 5 bars
Exit:   Trail@5pts/2.0×ATR (AM), Trail@4pts/1.5×ATR (PM), BE@4pts
Result: WR 65-68%, PF 4.5-4.9, +6.0-6.3/d (129d walk-forward)
File:   combos/cb.py

#2. NR7 — Narrowest Range 7 Bars 🔬
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Signal: Current bar range is smallest in last 7 bars + range < 0.8×ATR
Entry:  Breakout direction on next bar
Filter: ATR 2.5-4.5, time window AM/PM
Exit:   Standard trail (same as CB)
Notes:  Classic "NR7 day" concept adapted to intraday 5m. More selective
        than NR4 (fewer signals). Both directions.
Source: reddit_combos.md, backtest_20combos.py (COMBO 2)

#4. ORB — Opening Range Breakout ❌ REJECTED (2026-06-06)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Signal: First 3 bars form opening range (OR). Breakout above OR_high (BUY)
        or below OR_low (SELL) in bars 4-8.
Entry:  Break of OR high/low + 0.1 offset
Filter: OR range < 0.8×ATR, OR range >= 0.3 pts
Exit:   Standard session trail
Result: 5m: T=68, WR 32.4%, PF 0.82, -0.22/d (130d)
        Best filtered: T=15, WR 40%, PF 1.39, +0.12/d (not viable)
        3m/1m: all negative
WHY FAIL: VN30F1M lacks continuation after opening range. 38% trades are
        immediate fakeouts (MFE<2pts). No feature (ATR, RSI, ADX, OR ratio,
        candle body) can pre-identify SL trades — distributions overlap 100%.
        Problem is structural, not filterable.
Source: reddit_combos.md (Strategy 1), backtest_20combos.py (COMBO 4)
Analysis: research/analyze_orb_multitf.py, research/analyze_orb_sl_deep.py

#6. TTM Squeeze Fire ❌ REJECTED (2026-06-06)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Signal: Bollinger Bands inside Keltner Channel (squeeze ON) for 3+ bars,
        then squeeze releases (BB expands past KC). Direction from momentum.
Entry:  On first bar where squeeze turns OFF (BB exits KC)
Filter: Must have been in squeeze >= 3 bars, ATR 2.5-4.5
Exit:   Standard trail
Result: 5m: T=7/130d, WR 14%, PF 0.16, -0.16/d (too few signals)
        3m: T=5/25d, WR 20%, -0.45/d
        1m: T=10/25d, WR 30%, -0.27/d
WHY FAIL: Squeeze is ONLY a volatility predictor, carries ZERO directional
        info. Momentum/linreg predict direction < 44% (below coin flip).
        Signal also extremely rare on 5m (squeeze ON only 2.6% of bars).
        Could serve as CB volatility boost filter, not standalone.
Source: github_combos.md, backtest_20combos.py (COMBO 6)
Analysis: research/analyze_ttm_multitf.py

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Signal: CB compression on BOTH 5m AND 15m simultaneously
Entry:  Same as CB (breakout on 5m)

#9. Gap Fade ❌ REJECTED (2026-06-06)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Signal: AM open gaps > threshold from prior PM close → FADE the gap
Entry:  At AM open or after extension, direction opposite to gap
Filter: Gap ratio 0.5-2.5×ATR (too small = noise, too big = momentum)
Exit:   Standard trail or gap-fill based TP
Result: 5m stretched entry: T=53/130d, WR 64%, PF 2.2, +0.89/d (best)
        5m naive entry: T=123, WR 36%, PF 0.82, -0.54/d
        3m/1m: all negative
WHY FAIL: VN30F1M gaps are structural (median 7.4pts = overnight global
        adjustment over 18.5h), not inefficiencies. Fill rate only 58% for
        large gaps. Entry at open = momentum continuation (WR 17%).
        "Stretched entry" has micro-edge but 7× below CB and only 0.4
        trades/day. Not viable standalone.
Source: reddit_combos.md (Strategy 7), backtest_20combos.py (COMBO 9)
Analysis: research/analyze_gap_fade_multitf.py
